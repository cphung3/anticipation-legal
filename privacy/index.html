Privacy policy

ANTicipation — countdown to joy · Last updated 28 July 2026

This is the whole policy. It is short because the app does very little with your data, and we would rather you actually read it.

The short version

ANTicipation keeps everything on your phone. It works completely, forever, with no account and no internet connection. If you sign in, a copy is also kept in the cloud so a new phone can get your meadow back. Nothing is sold, nothing is shared with advertisers, and there is no tracking of any kind in this app.

What we keep, and where

On your phone, always. Your countdowns, your moods, your notes and ratings, your preferences, and any photos you attach. Photos in particular never leave the device — only the file path is stored, and that path is never uploaded.

In the cloud, if you are signed in. Your countdown titles, dates, categories, notes and ratings, and your daily moods, stored under an account identifier belonging only to you. This exists so that signing in on a new phone restores what you were waiting for.

Before you sign in. The app still creates an anonymous account identifier on first launch, so that a cloud copy has somewhere to live. It is not linked to your name, your email, or anything else about you.

If you sign in with Apple or Google. We receive an email address and, if the provider supplies one, a display name. They are used to recognise your account and for nothing else. We never email you.

Sharing a countdown

When you share a countdown, the people you invite can see its title, its date, your display name, and — if you have logged one — your most recent mood emoji. That is the entire list.

Only people you gave the six-character code to can join. A shared countdown holds at most twelve people. There is no public feed, no way to find other users, no comments, and no messaging. Leaving a shared countdown removes your name and mood from it immediately.

Poster art

When you create a countdown for a film or a show, its title is sent to TMDB to look for poster art. The title is all that is sent — no account identifier, no device identifier, nothing that connects the search to you. If no key is configured in the build, this does not happen at all.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Crash reports

Release builds send crash reports and caught errors to Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix what breaks. These contain diagnostic information — the error, the stack trace, the device model, the OS version — and not the contents of your countdowns.

What we never do

We do not collect your location. We do not read your contacts. We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, or trackers of any kind. We do not build a profile of you. We do not sell or rent your data to anyone, and we do not share it with data brokers. There are no ads in this app.

Deleting your data

Two things live in Settings, on the You screen, and both do exactly what they say:

  • Delete all my data removes every countdown, mood, shared-countdown membership, preference and saved photo from your phone and from the cloud, and cancels every pending notification.
  • Delete my account does all of that and then deletes the account itself.

Both are immediate and permanent. We keep no backup copy for you to recover from — that is the point of them.

The same instructions, including how to request deletion without the app, are published at the account-deletion page.

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the phone. If you were signed in, the cloud copy remains until you delete it, so use "delete all my data" first if that is what you want.

Children

ANTicipation is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Where the data lives

Cloud data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Crashlytics), which may process it on servers outside your country. Everything is encrypted in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what happens to your data, the date at the top will change and the app will say so before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions, or a request to see or delete your data: privacy@anticipation.app
