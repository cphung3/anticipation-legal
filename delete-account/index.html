Delete an ANTicipation account

You do not need to sign in to use ANTicipation. When Firebase is configured, the app creates an anonymous account identifier on first launch. You can request deletion of the active Firebase account and its data in the app; if you connected Apple or Google, the app may ask you to verify with that provider.

Delete inside the app

  1. Open You.
  2. In Your trail is backed up, choose Delete my account.
  3. Confirm the permanent deletion.
  4. If you signed in with Apple or Google, complete the verification prompt.

This removes the Firebase Authentication account, cloud countdowns and moods, owned shared countdowns, memberships in other shared countdowns, the on-device copy, preferences, saved countdown photos, and scheduled notifications.

Request deletion without the app

Email anticipationapp@gmail.com from the email address connected to the account and use the subject Delete my ANTicipation account. Include the display name shown in the app. We may ask for provider verification so one person cannot delete another person's account.

Account data is deleted rather than deactivated. We do not retain a recovery backup. Firebase and store-provider operational logs may remain only for the limited period required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance; they are not used to restore the account.

Uninstalling the app clears device data but does not itself delete a signed-in cloud account.
